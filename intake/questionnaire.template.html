Before the first problem

Tell the Loom how your institution works, and where that is written.

Nine blocks, one per accountable role. Answer only your block. Every question asks two things: what is true here, and which document says so. That second field decides what happens to your answer.

  • Sourced: you named a document, register or signed decision. Only these reach a BrainKit section.
  • Claimed: you answered from experience. It goes to the gap register with your role as owner, to be written down.
  • Unknown: left blank. It goes to the gap register for escalation.
  • Roles, never names. Nothing you type here is approved by typing it. Your answers are saved in this browser and exported as a file the facilitator imports.
