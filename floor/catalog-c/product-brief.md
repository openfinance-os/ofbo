# Product brief — <PB-nnn>

> ## ⛔ WRITE CLASS · `lives-on-the-floor` — NEVER FROZEN
>
> This page **lives on the floor**. It is never frozen into `discovery/runs/`, no gate ever reads
> it, and no export ever checks it. That cuts both ways: write freely, and understand that
> **nothing downstream will catch what you write here**. On this page you are the control.
>
> **PII rule — before you type, not after.** No names. No contact details. No id, account or
> reference numbers. No verbatim customer detail — not even "just one line, to illustrate".
> People appear as **segments and roles**, never as individuals; a complaint or an incident that
> prompted this brief is described as a **category**, never as the case. If you cannot write it
> without naming someone, it does not belong on the floor at all.
>
> A brief is a **sketch, not a commitment**. When it hardens it does not get frozen — it gets
> rewritten as `discovery/templates/problem-statement.md`, and *that* artifact freezes and faces
> the D-gates. Copying this page into `discovery/runs/` is the single move this write class
> forbids, and `scripts/floor-only-check.mjs` reports it as a crossing if it happens.
