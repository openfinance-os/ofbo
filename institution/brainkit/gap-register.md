# OFBO Institutional BrainKit — draft gap register

The Loom 2.4.1 BrainKit scaffold is installed, but it is not institutional policy and is not
approved. No approved institutional source pack or legal institution was supplied for this
repository-level adoption, so the neutral templates remain deliberately unfilled.

Before an OFBO bank pilot can name an institution profile, accountable humans must resolve:

- the adopting legal institution and Institutional BrainKit id;
- approved sources for identity, terminology, architecture, technology policy, governance, and strategy;
- human owners for every BrainKit section, mapped to the institution's identity registry;
- the authority and source record that ratifies the Kosli provider selection;
- the OFBO brand projection the institution wants to inherit or override;
- review, approval, effective, expiry, revocation, and distribution decisions; and
- section/package sealing after—not before—the source and owner decisions are complete.

Until then, `institution/brainkit/manifest.json` stays `draft`, its approvals stay empty, and the
existing OFBO discovery brand remains the active D7 seam. This register is a human review agenda,
not evidence that any gap is closed.
