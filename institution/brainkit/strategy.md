## How a run cites an intent

- `problem-statement.md` → **Strategic intent** line: `SI-nn`, or *"none recorded"*.
- `business-case.md` §1 → the same id; a case for a problem serving no intent says so.
- No gate reads `SI-*` yet (2.2.0). The citation is prose the facilitator and the decision
  authority read; the mechanical check is a later step, and this section says so rather than
  implying it.
